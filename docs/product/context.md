# FACT Indonesia Context

FACT Indonesia is the business reference for this portfolio: a training and
consultancy organization concerned with individual development, organizational
capability, creativity, and practical entrepreneurship.

Its official [organization profile](https://fact-indonesia.com/profile/)
provides the context for the case study. Its [homepage](https://fact-indonesia.com/)
provides the published service categories recorded in
[`factIndonesia.json`](../../resources/js/Data/factIndonesia.json).

## Intended Audiences

- Individuals comparing learning and development opportunities.
- Employers exploring support for staff and career transitions.
- Entrepreneurs seeking training and business guidance.
- Organizations evaluating consultancy and community programs.

## Product Direction

The proposed digital journey is to understand the organization, compare its
services, inspect program information, and reach an appropriate contact path.
These are design goals, not claims that the inherited source implements
course enrollment, learning delivery, or certificate issuance.

The shared code currently retains RoyalVilla's property, account, payment,
and moderation terminology. Adaptation requires explicit domain work rather
than relabeling property records as courses.
